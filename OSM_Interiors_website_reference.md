## Lead capture and conversion flow

The site repeatedly invites visitors to **“Meet a Designer,” “Book Free Design Session,”** or **“Get Free Estimate.”** The intake form asks for:

- Name, email, and mobile number
- Where the visitor needs interiors
- Home size
- Preferred budget range
- Project thoughts and ideas

The project page also presents a portfolio of apartment projects, with project names and YouTube walkthrough links. That portfolio and the breadth of service/locality pages are useful context for a personalized demo or outreach message.

Sources: [Home](https://osminteriors.com/), [Projects](https://osminteriors.com/our-project/), [Contact](https://osminteriors.com/contact-us/).

## Useful demo implications

1. Align chatbot questions with the existing form: project locality, home size, budget, and project description, then collect contact details and preferred follow-up method.
2. Offer a human handoff for detailed design, materials, timelines, warranty, and pricing questions; the public site does not give complete terms for those answers.
3. Keep the dashboard's lead record aligned with those fields so submissions from the form, chatbot, and (if later added) phone receptionist use one structure.
4. The site already has multiple CTAs and a project portfolio, so the demo should show how an enquiry is captured and followed through rather than presenting the chatbot as a replacement for the current site.
